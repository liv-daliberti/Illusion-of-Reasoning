To test whether this pattern is specific to small GRPO-tuned models, we evaluate DeepSeek\textendash R1 and GPT\textendash 4o under matched decoding conditions on RHour. As shown in Table~\ref{tab:external-models-carpark}, both models exhibit \emph{very low} canonical shift rates across temperatures (10.53--11.45\% for DeepSeek\textendash R1 and 1.45--3.12\% for GPT\textendash 4o), and accuracy conditioned on a shift shows no systematic benefit, suggesting that the phenomenon generalizes across model families and training paradigms.
